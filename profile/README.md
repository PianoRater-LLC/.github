# PianoRater, LLC

**Building PianoRate/r — a better way to discover and review pianos at venues.**

PianoRater, LLC is the company behind **PianoRate/r**, a platform being developed to help pianists find venues, learn about the instruments available, and share firsthand feedback about their condition.

## 🎹 PianoRate/r

We're building a place where pianists can:

- Discover venues and the pianos they have available.
- Read and share reviews about tuning, key response, pedals, and overall condition.
- Explore piano details, ratings, and photos.
- Help keep information useful and up to date for other musicians.

Future plans include tools for venue owners to manage piano information and share maintenance updates.

## 🚀 Development

We're starting with a **web-first MVP** using Expo Web, with native iOS and Android apps as potential future releases.

Our planned technology stack includes:

- **Frontend:** React Native, Expo, TypeScript
- **Backend:** Rust, Axum
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, SQLx

PianoRate/r is currently in development. Features and implementation details may change as we build and test the platform.
